Yemeni Fahsa

Description

Fahsa is a widely popular traditional Yemeni dish and one of the most famous and iconic dishes in Yemeni cuisine.

Ingredients

  • 4 white part of spring onions
  • 4 garlic cloves
  • 2 tomatoes
  • 3 chilies (optional)
  • 1 kg beef chunks
  • tbsps tomato puree
  • salt & pepper
  • 1 tsp coriander powder
  • 1 tsp cardamom powder
  • 1 tsp cinnamon powder
  • 4 cups of water

Steps

  1. start by chopping the spring onion, the garlic, and the tomatoes. Sauté the onions and garlic in a drizzle of olive oil and add the chili.. Once the smell fills the kitchen, add the beef chunks and brown the beef for about 5 to 6 minutes.

  2. add all the seasonings with the tomato purée and the chopped tomatoes and stir them for another five minutes then add the 4 cups of water.

  3. once the pot bubbles drop the heat to medium low cover and let it cook for about an hour and a half the meat is soft.

  4. add the meat to a skillet and shred it with a fork. Give the meat, a hefty pinch of salt and a cup of the sauce.

  5. stir it very gently, and once it starts to bubble top it with the creamy sahawuq helba.

  6. Serve it with some bread and enjoy.
